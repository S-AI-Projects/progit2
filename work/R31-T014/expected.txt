Before pushing topic:
  local refs/heads/main is C; remote refs/heads/main is C.
  remote HEAD is symbolic to refs/heads/main.
  remote refs/heads/topic does not exist yet.

After `git push -u origin topic` while on local topic:
  local refs/heads/topic remains B; local refs/heads/main remains C.
  remote refs/heads/topic is created at B; remote refs/heads/main remains C.
  local origin/topic tracks B and origin/main tracks C.
  remote HEAD remains symbolic to refs/heads/main.
  Local HEAD is symbolic to refs/heads/topic and the worktree is clean.

Commit IDs are captured dynamically. Exact refs and command output are in actual.txt.
